Settle the island, trade with rivals, and sail for what lies past the map.

Play in the browser Nothing to install and nothing to trust. Send friends the link and a room code. Download for Windows Installer, per-user — no administrator rights needed. Portable build A single file that runs without installing anything.

What you get

  • Every expansion, 21 scenarios Base game, Seafarers, Cities & Knights, Explorers & Pirates and Traders & Barbarians.
  • Two to six players, or on your own Everyone joins from their own device with a five-letter code — or pass and play on one screen against bots.
  • No account, no server, no cost Games run directly between players' devices, end-to-end encrypted. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.

Worth knowing before you download

  • Windows will warn you on first run. The build is unsigned — a certificate costs about $200 a year for a game nobody is charged for. Choose More info → Run anyway, or play in the browser and skip the question.
  • Multiplayer needs internet, but not a server. Two browsers are introduced over a public relay and then talk directly. Pass-and-play on one machine works with no connection at all.
  • Everyone needs the same version. The wire protocol is not versioned, so an out-of-date copy cannot play with an up-to-date one. The installed build checks for updates on launch.
